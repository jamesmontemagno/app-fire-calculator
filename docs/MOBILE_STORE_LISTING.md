## Description

My Fire # helps you explore financial-independence scenarios with 14 focused
calculators, including Standard, Coast, Lean, Fat, Barista, and Reverse FIRE,
plus withdrawal rate, savings and investment rate, debt payoff, healthcare gap,
72(t) / SEPP, Roth conversion strategy, interest, and retirement cash flow.

Track what you actually own. Add your accounts, income, expenses, and debts once,
then link any calculator to your profile so it always starts from real numbers
instead of guesses.

See your progress over time. Run a monthly check-in to snapshot your balances,
then open History & trends for net worth, assets versus debts, and per-account
charts built entirely from your own check-ins.

Build and compare named plans, adjust common assumptions, visualize projections,
and export accessible Excel workbooks. Privacy Mode hides every dollar amount
with one tap when you are somewhere public. Calculations are estimates for
educational purposes and are not financial advice.

Financial inputs, drafts, plans, and quiz answers stay in local app storage. The
app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or automatic data upload. The
operating system may include app data in an encrypted device backup when the user
enables platform backups. Backup export and legal links run only after an explicit
user action.

## Review Notes

The app is fully usable without an account or network connection. On first launch,
reviewers may complete or skip the FIRE Quiz. All 14 calculators are available from
the Calculators tab. The Accounts tab holds accounts, income, expenses, and debts,
along with the monthly check-in and History & trends. Profile stores shared
assumptions that calculators can link to. Settings contains theme, accessibility,
calculator defaults, currency, Privacy Mode, local backup/restore, legal links, and
confirmed data deletion.
